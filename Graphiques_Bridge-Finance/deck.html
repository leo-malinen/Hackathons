The problem

Unexpected expenses destabilise otherwise functional finances.

A person with steady income, controlled essentials and a growing buffer can still be one repair away from a hard year. The problem is liquidity at a moment, not behaviour over a lifetime.

04 / 12

Why current options fall short

Every existing answer solves a different problem.

Credit and overdrafts

Fast and available. A median $35 overdraft fee, or a revolving balance with no end date.

High-cost emergency credit

A median 391 percent APR, with borrowers indebted a median 199 days a year.

Budgeting and education

Improve long-term behaviour. Neither pays the mechanic on Thursday.

05 / 12

The solution

Bridge

A financial resilience platform that combines emergency support, affordability assessment, recovery planning and savings rebuilding into one experience, delivered through organisations that already serve the people who need it.

A financial emergency should be treated as a recovery problem, not simply a lending opportunity.

08 / 12

The innovation

One extra question.

“Can this person repay?”

Produces a loan, a rate and a schedule.

“What would help this person recover?”

Produces support sized to the gap, a plan sized to capacity, a savings restart, and a way to tell whether any of it worked.

09 / 12

The business model

Business to business to consumer.

Employers, universities, nonprofits and financial institutions establish Resilience Funds and pay for the platform that runs them. Revenue comes from institutional contracts, not from interest charged to people in difficulty.

Bridge is a concept designed and submitted for the Graphiques Innovation Challenge. It is a proposal, not a product: it is not a live financial service, it does not lend money, it has no users, partners or revenue, and nothing here is financial advice. The Bridge Resilience Profile is a conceptual product framework for financial resilience planning. It is not a credit score and does not represent a lending decision. Every scenario, dashboard and pilot figure on this site is clearly labelled as illustrative. External statistics carry a named source, a year and a link. Any real implementation would require appropriate financial, legal, regulatory, compliance, consumer protection, privacy and risk review.

30 to 45 seconds · The intervention

Most financial products help people transact. Bridge is designed to help people recover.

Traditional credit asks whether a person can repay. Bridge adds a second question: what would help this person recover? That question changes the product from a loan into a recovery plan with support attached.
