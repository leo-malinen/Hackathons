Privacy

Your settings stay in your browser.

A tool that watches how you read is a tool that could learn things about you that you never chose to tell anyone. That is a real risk and it deserves a plain answer rather than a policy. Here is the answer: everything is local, nothing is inferred, and one press deletes it all.

What is kept and what is never collected

What is kept, on your device

  • Your profiles: a name and a set of settings.
  • Which sites you have set differently, by their address only.
  • How many times you changed a setting yourself, as a number.
  • Which suggestions you have already turned down.
  • Which pages you have switched the Copilot off on.
  • The language service key, if you added one, and what it has cost you.

What is never collected

  • The text of the pages you read.
  • What you type into any form, ever.
  • Passwords, card numbers, or anything in a protected field.
  • Your browsing history.
  • Any guess about your disability, diagnosis, age, or health.
  • Page content used to train a model. Not by default, not quietly, not at all.

The one thing that leaves

Rewriting is the only part that goes anywhere.

Making text bigger happens on your machine. Rewriting a paragraph into plainer words may not. So this is the part to be careful about, and these are the rules it runs under.

Only when you ask

Nothing is sent because a page loaded. It is sent because you selected a passage and pressed a button. Every time.

Only that passage

The passage you picked, and the least context needed to make sense of it. Not the page, not the site, not what you were doing before.

Never a field you filled

Form values, passwords, and payment fields are excluded before anything is packed up. This one is not a setting you can switch off by mistake.

How it works right now

You bring the key, or nothing leaves at all.

Rewriting is switched off until you put a key in yourself, under Language in the panel. There is no key inside this site and no account behind it. Until you add one, the panel says it is off and not a single word goes anywhere.

  • The key is written into this browser and nowhere else. Not into the site, not onto a server, not into the file you downloaded. Forget the key removes it.
  • The first time you ask for a rewrite you are shown what is about to be sent, and you can say no. Shown once, then it gets out of your way.
  • Only the passage goes. Not the page, not its address, not the site you are on, not what you were doing before.
  • Anything you typed cannot be sent at all. Not by confirming, not by accident. The option does not appear on a form field.
  • Payment, sign-in, legal, medical, and emergency wording asks first, every time, and shows you the words before it moves them.
  • The panel shows what you have spent as you go, because a service you are paying for should not be quiet about it.

Whether a shipped product would run this on your own machine instead is still open, and on-device would be the better answer. What is built today is the version above, and this page describes that rather than a plan.

Not yet built

If settings ever follow you between devices.

There is no account and no sync today. If it is ever built, these are the terms it has to meet, written down now while nobody is under pressure to ship it.

  • Off unless you switch it on. Never on by default, never on after an update.
  • Encrypted on the way and at rest. Settings only. Never page content.
  • Exportable and deletable from the same panel, with the deletion actually reaching the server.
  • Still nothing inferred. Sync moves a settings file. It does not build a picture of you.
