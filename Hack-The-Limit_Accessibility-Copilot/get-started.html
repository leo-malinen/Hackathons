Open the demo

Install it now

It runs on real websites today.

Not a plan for later. One file, installed in a couple of clicks through a userscript manager, and from then on the Copilot is on every site you visit. Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, and Safari, because a userscript does not care which one you use.

Get a userscript manager

Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey. Both are free, both are in the extension stores, and either one will do.

Open the script and press install

Your manager notices the address ends in .user.js, opens its own install screen, and shows you the whole script before anything runs. Read it if you like. It is one file with nothing hidden in it.

Install the userscript

Go to any website

Press the Copilot button at the bottom right, or hold Alt and press A. Pick a profile, or open Controls and set one thing at a time.

Select any sentence and a small bar offers to put it in plain words. That part needs a key of your own, under Language, and stays off until you add one.

Change your mind whenever you like

Restore original puts the page back. Turn off here switches it off for one site. Delete all of it clears every setting on every site. None of that needs a settings screen or a confirmation you have to hunt for.

What it asks for, and why

Three permissions, each with a reason.

  • Run on every site. It cannot change a page it is not allowed to read. Your manager can narrow this to a list of sites you choose, and the Copilot has its own Turn off here for one site at a time.
  • Store settings. Your profiles, your per-site settings, and the key if you add one. They live in the manager's own storage on your machine. Nothing is sent anywhere and there is no account.
  • Reach one address on the internet. Only openrouter.ai, only when you have picked a passage and asked for it. That single host is written into the script's header, so your manager will refuse anything else.

Before you install it, read what it cannot do. It is a short list and it is better read first than found out later.

The closed beta

Choose how you want the web to work.

The beta will be small on purpose, and weighted towards people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnification, and plain language every day. Say which describes you if you want to, or say nothing.

Beta participants are paid for their time, and nobody is asked to name a diagnosis to take part.
