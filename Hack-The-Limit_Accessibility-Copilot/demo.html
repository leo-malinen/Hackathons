Residents' parking: the new scheme

Published 26 August · Parking and streets

Original

The council has published the new residents' parking scheme and is inviting representations from affected households, which must be received no later than the final day of the current month, whether submitted in writing to the address printed on the notice or via the online representation form.

Households presently in possession of a valid permit are not required to submit a further application, notwithstanding which the council requests that all permit holders verify that the particulars held on record remain accurate.

Plain language

The council has published the new parking scheme. If it affects you, tell them what you think by the end of this month. You can write to the address on the notice or use the form on this site.

Already have a permit? You do not need to apply again. The council does ask you to check that your details are still right. A rewrite can drop detail. Press below to read the original wording.

      How to read what you just saw

      The three kinds of change.

      People often mistake a transformed page for a redesigned one. These are three different things, and the Copilot keeps them apart on purpose.

      One

      How it looks

      Size, spacing, colour, contrast, and movement. These are style changes only. Not one word of the page is different, and nothing is added or removed.

      Two

      How it reads

      A plain-language version of a passage, shown with the original beside it and a note that a rewrite can drop detail. This one is never automatic.

      Three

      How it is built

      A skip link, numbered headings, drawn regions, a stronger focus ring. Help for keyboards and screen readers. It never moves a control, because moving one can change what a task means.
